      How it plays

      Maps are about 60% monster fights, with elites, shops, campfires and mini-games mixed in. Each room is a short riding challenge. Some ask you to hold a power band, some to sprint, hit a cadence, outrun a boulder, or win a tug-of-war. Fail and you lose HP. You earn gold, potions and relics along the way.
      The trainer switches between ERG (it sets the watts and you control cadence) and SIM (it sets a gradient and you use your gears).
      You recover on the map at an easy ERG between rooms.
